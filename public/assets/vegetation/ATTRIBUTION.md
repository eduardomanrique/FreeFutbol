# Vegetation assets

Redistributed under CC0 1.0: https://polyhaven.com/license

- Fern 02 model and 1K diffuse, normal, ARM and alpha textures: https://polyhaven.com/a/fern_02
- Palm Bark 1K color, normal and roughness maps: https://polyhaven.com/a/palm_bark

Downloaded from Poly Haven on 2026-09-24. Fern variants are scaled and rotated
for planters and adapted into palm crowns. Palm trunks are generated curved
meshes. The separate alpha map restores leaf cutouts omitted by the JPEG glTF
export. All files are served locally and bundled for offline mobile use.
